SECTION: Fixed Deposits
SecureBank offers Fixed Deposit (FD) accounts with the following interest rates:
- 7 days to 45 days: 4.50% per annum
- 46 days to 90 days: 5.25% per annum
- 91 days to 180 days: 5.75% per annum
- 181 days to 1 year: 6.50% per annum
- 1 year to 2 years: 7.00% per annum
- 2 years to 5 years: 7.25% per annum
Senior citizens receive an additional 0.50% on all FD tenures.
Premature withdrawal is allowed with a 1% penalty on the applicable rate.
Minimum FD amount is Rs. 10,000. Maximum is Rs. 2 crore per customer.

SECTION: Savings Account
SecureBank savings accounts offer 3.50% per annum interest on daily balance.
Minimum Average Monthly Balance (AMB) is Rs. 10,000 for urban branches.
Minimum AMB for rural branches is Rs. 2,000.
Non-maintenance of AMB attracts a charge of Rs. 500 per month.
Free transactions: 5 ATM withdrawals per month at any bank.
Beyond free limit: Rs. 20 per ATM transaction.
Internet banking and mobile banking are free for all savings account holders.

SECTION: Home Loan
SecureBank home loans are available at interest rates starting from 8.50% per annum.
Loan tenure: 5 years to 30 years.
Maximum loan amount: Up to 80% of property value (LTV ratio).
Minimum income requirement: Rs. 25,000 per month for salaried individuals.
Documents required for home loan application:
- Identity proof: Aadhaar card, PAN card, or passport
- Address proof: Utility bill or Aadhaar card
- Income proof: Last 3 months salary slips and Form 16
- Bank statements: Last 6 months
- Property documents: Sale agreement and title deed
Processing fee: 0.5% of loan amount, maximum Rs. 15,000.
Pre-payment charges: Nil for floating rate loans.

SECTION: Personal Loan
Personal loans available from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 25 lakhs.
Interest rate: 10.50% to 18.00% per annum depending on credit score.
Tenure: 1 year to 5 years.
Minimum CIBIL score required: 700.
Processing fee: 2% of loan amount.
Documents: Aadhaar, PAN, last 3 salary slips, 6 months bank statement.
Disbursement within 48 hours for eligible applicants.

SECTION: Credit Card
SecureBank offers three credit card variants: Classic, Gold, and Platinum.
Classic card: Annual fee Rs. 500, credit limit up to Rs. 1 lakh.
Gold card: Annual fee Rs. 1,000, credit limit up to Rs. 3 lakhs, 2x reward points on dining.
Platinum card: Annual fee Rs. 2,500, credit limit up to Rs. 10 lakhs, airport lounge access.
Minimum payment due: 5% of outstanding balance or Rs. 200, whichever is higher.
Late payment charges: Rs. 500 for bills up to Rs. 10,000; Rs. 1,000 above Rs. 10,000.
Interest on revolving credit: 3.5% per month (42% per annum).
Cash advance fee: 2.5% of amount withdrawn, minimum Rs. 500.

SECTION: EMI and Loan Repayment
Missed EMI charge: Rs. 500 flat fee plus 2% penal interest per month on overdue amount.
Bounce charge for ECS/NACH: Rs. 500 per instance.
Loan foreclosure: Allowed after 6 months; no charges on floating rate loans.
Foreclosure charges on fixed rate loans: 2% of outstanding principal.
EMI date change: Allowed once per year, Rs. 500 processing fee.

SECTION: Fraud and Security
If you suspect unauthorized transactions, immediately call 1800-XXX-XXXX (24x7).
Block your debit or credit card instantly via the SecureBank mobile app under Card Controls.
Report fraud within 3 days for zero liability protection under RBI guidelines.
Reporting after 3 days but within 7 days: customer liability capped at Rs. 10,000.
Reporting after 7 days: liability determined case by case.
Do not share OTP, CVV, or PIN with anyone including bank employees.
SecureBank will never ask for your OTP or full card number over phone or email.

SECTION: Account Opening
To open a savings account, visit any branch with:
- PAN card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar card for KYC
- Two passport-size photographs
- Initial deposit of Rs. 10,000 (urban) or Rs. 2,000 (rural)
Online account opening available via the SecureBank website with video KYC.
Account activation takes 1-2 business days for online applications.

SECTION: NEFT RTGS IMPS
NEFT: Available 24x7. No minimum amount. Charges: Rs. 2 to Rs. 25 depending on amount.
RTGS: Minimum Rs. 2 lakhs. Available 7am to 6pm on working days. Charges: Rs. 25 to Rs. 50.
IMPS: Available 24x7. Up to Rs. 5 lakhs per transaction. Charges: Rs. 5 to Rs. 15.
UPI: Free, up to Rs. 1 lakh per transaction, Rs. 2 lakhs for verified merchants.
Note: The AI assistant cannot initiate any of these transfers. Visit branch or use the app.
